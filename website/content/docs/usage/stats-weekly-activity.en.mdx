The **Weekly Activity** section shows how your logged time is spread across the week of the selected day, as a bar chart.

<div align="center">![Weekly activity](/images/stats-weekly-activity.png)</div>

## Reading the chart

- One **bar per day**, Monday to Sunday.
- Each bar's height is proportional to the time logged that day.
- The **selected day** is highlighted.
- A tooltip shows details (date and duration) when hovering a bar.

## Goal reference

A reference line marks your **daily work target**, making it easy to compare each day against the expected goal at a glance.

## Link with the calendar

The displayed week always matches the week of the day selected in the [calendar](/en/docs/utilisation/stats-calendar). Select another day to change the analyzed week.
